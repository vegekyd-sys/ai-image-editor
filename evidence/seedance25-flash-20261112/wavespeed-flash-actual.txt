Seedance 2.5 Flash on WaveSpeedAI: Specs, Access, and When to Use It
Seedance 2.5 Flash, ByteDance's faster Seedance 2.5 tier announced on November 12, 2026, is available on WaveSpeedAI. Here is what was announced, how to call it, and when to use Flash or Seedance 2.5.

Seedance 2.5 Flash is a new, faster tier of ByteDance’s Seedance 2.5 video model. ByteDance’s Volcano Engine announced it on November 12, 2026, as the model closest in quality to Seedance 2.5, with generation speed doubled and a lower price.

What Did ByteDance Announce?
According to ByteDance’s announcement as reported by

generation is twice as fast as Seedance 2.5.

lower than Seedance 2.5, by about a third at 720p text-to-video and image-to-video.

What Should You Not Assume?
Announcement numbers are a starting point, not a test result:
That ByteDance’s “2x faster” holds for your prompts.
Speed depends on duration, resolution, references and queue time. Measure on your own shots.
That Flash matches Seedance 2.5 on every feature.
Flash tops out at 720p, so 1080p and 4K work still belongs to Seedance 2.5. Check duration, references, audio and editing one by one when you test it.
That a lower price means a lower project cost.
What matters is the cost of an accepted result, including the takes you throw away.
That “Flash” means the same thing as “Fast”, “Mini” or “Turbo”.
These are different tiers with different limits. Compare them by their published settings, not by their names.

A Draft-Then-Final Workflow
Flash is aimed at a simple way of working: iterate cheaply, then render the keeper once.
Draft on Flash.
Use Seedance 2.5 Flash at 480p or 720p with a short duration to settle the idea: framing, action, camera move and sound.
Change one thing per take.
Compare a static camera against a slow push-in, or two lighting setups. One variable per take makes the choice obvious.
Lock the prompt and references.
Once a take works, stop editing the prompt.
Render the final once.
For 1080p or 4K delivery, send the same prompt and references to Seedance 2.5. If 720p is enough, the Flash take is already your final.
The request is the same for both steps. Only the model ID and the resolution change:

How to Test Flash Against Your Current Model
Keep the model ID configurable.
If the model is a setting and not a hardcoded string, switching to Flash is a one-line change.
Save a small test set.
Pick 10 to 20 prompts that look like your real work: a product shot, a dialogue scene, a fast action shot, a scene with several characters. Keep the reference files with them.
Record a baseline.
For each prompt, note the time from request to result, the cost, and whether the take was usable on your current model. Without those numbers you cannot tell what Flash improves.
Write down your acceptance checks.
Subject stays consistent, the action completes, the framing is stable, the last frame is usable. Score every model against the same list.
How to Judge Flash
Run the same test set on Flash and on the model you use now, with matching duration, resolution and references wherever both support them. Then compare three numbers:
Time to a usable clip
, including queue time and retries, not only generation time.
Usable-output rate
: clips that pass every check divided by clips generated.
Cost per accepted clip
: total spend divided by the clips you would actually ship.
A model that is twice as fast but needs twice as many takes has not saved anything. Use several representative shots, not one favorable example.
FAQ
Is Seedance 2.5 Flash released?
It was announced by ByteDance on November 12, 2026, and is rolling out on ByteDance’s own apps and partner platforms. Its API is scheduled to open on Volcano Ark on November 15.
Is Seedance 2.5 Flash available on WaveSpeedAI?
Yes. Seedance 2.5 Flash text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video and video-edit are available, alongside Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.5 Turbo, Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast and Seedance 2.0 Mini.
How is Flash different from Seedance 2.5?
ByteDance positions Flash as twice as fast and lower in price, with 480p and 720p output, while Seedance 2.5 keeps 1080p, 4K and Draft mode for the highest-quality work.
Will Seedance 2.5 Flash be cheaper than Seedance 2.5?
ByteDance says Flash is priced lower, and on WaveSpeedAI Flash is priced below Seedance 2.5 at the same resolution. Check each model page for current prices, compare at the same duration and resolution, and count the takes you discard.
Is Seedance 2.5 Turbo the same as Seedance 2.5 Flash?
No. Seedance 2.5 Turbo is a faster, more affordable Seedance 2.5 tier with 720p and 1080p output. Flash is a separate model with 480p and 720p output.
What is the fastest way to use Seedance today?
Draft with
Seedance 2.5 Turbo
at 720p, then render the final take with
Seedance 2.5
at 1080p or 4K.
Details about Seedance 2.5 Flash come from ByteDance’s announcement as reported by news outlets and may change. Recheck the official documentation and the model pages before planning production work around it.
