# Cinematic Video

Use for trailers, teasers, title sequences, product launch films, atmospheric
shorts, and story-led pieces where image, edit rhythm, and sound carry meaning.
Do not route ordinary tutorials or quick social clips here.

## Required Directors

Read `skills/_shared/studio-production/production-contract.md`,
`skills/_shared/studio-production/audio-direction.md`,
`skills/_shared/studio-production/review-contract.md`, and
`skills/_shared/remotion-director-contract.md` before planning. If a reference
is supplied, also read the reference analysis director.

## Workflow

1. Start recipe `cinematic-video` and lock runtime, format, duration, narration,
   and source/generated/hybrid mode.
2. In the brief name the dramatic question, viewer feeling, image strategy,
   sound strategy, and final emotional turn.
3. The proposal must offer at least two genuinely different cinematic engines,
   such as suspenseful reveal, poetic montage, escalating trailer, or intimate
   observational story. Palette changes alone do not count.
4. Prefer atelier mode for hero work. Each scene needs a shot intention, scale,
   movement, duration, sound role, and transition reason.
5. Replace mood adjectives with visible causes: lens/framing, light, texture,
   blocking, edit rhythm, and audio behavior.
6. Generate only assets named in the storyboard. Use longer hero holds and
   shorter connective shots; avoid a uniform metronome edit.
7. Compose in Remotion with restrained text and a small transition vocabulary.
8. Review the real MP4 for blank frames, weak source crops, repeated layouts,
   audio balance, and whether the ending lands.

## Quality Bar

The video should still communicate its intent with most text hidden. It must
feel shot-led rather than like presentation slides wearing a film grain filter.
