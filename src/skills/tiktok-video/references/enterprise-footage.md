# Source-led films for business buyers

Choose one question that the supplied ranges answer on screen. Find the shot
that answers it before choosing the opening. A manifest row may include an
earlier editor's proposed hook or cut position; use its observed action and
source range as evidence. An overview of every department is optional.

Give the seed a concrete editing decision. For a replay, name the action the
second pass reveals. For a guessing game, choose the first shot that conceals
the object's identity and the later shot that reveals it. For a sample
comparison, pick two visible finishes and give the buyer enough time to see
the difference. Carry that decision into the middle and the ending; a new
headline over the same factory tour will produce the same film. Before VO,
choose which supplied clips to leave out. For a sample choice, compare the
finishes at a similar scale, give the viewer time to choose, then reveal the
names. The last frame can hold the choices together. For a handling replay,
end on the observable result. Include a production line, carton or sample-book
invitation only when it advances the chosen question or fulfills the brief.

Before generating VO, check each product claim in the script, hook and CTA.
Use facts the client supplied or an action the source actually shows:
- A hand bends this swatch or pulls this seam. A single demonstration cannot
  establish crack resistance, a load rating or the performance of another finish.
- A backing peels and a sample goes into a book. A sample-book shot does not
  establish suitability for walls, doors or every panel.
- A warehouse contains stacked cartons. Ownership of a production line,
  shipment readiness, lead times, certifications and stock require their own evidence.

Remove unsupported claims before recording. Keep uncertainty about different
samples visible in the edit: pair a close-up with its matching wider shot,
or show two samples as a comparison. For a sales close, use an invitation
connected to the footage, such as asking which finish the viewer wants to see
next. Preserve a specific CTA supplied by the client.
